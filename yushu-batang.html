巴塘河谷

Southern Batang Valley Loop

River gravel bars at first light, then south on the G214 to the Batang grassland for snowfinches and raptors, back north to Princess Wencheng Temple and the Leba Valley. A morning loop that returns you to town for lunch — or keeps you out all day if the valley delivers.

Coordinates & Amap 高德地图

Each stop has tap-to-copy buttons: one for the coordinates, one for the name in 汉字 — paste either into Amap's search box. Amap expects GCJ-02 coordinates. Most pins below came from Google's places data and are already GCJ-02-shifted, so they'll land right. The one stop tagged WGS-84 is a raw GPS reading — expect it to sit roughly 100–500 m off in Amap. Nothing has been converted; each pin is labeled with its datum.

The Lebagou pin is approximate — it's a valley birded from the road starting at the temple, not a point destination.

勒巴沟 Lèbā Gōu Leba Valley · starts at the temple Starts at stop 5 · ≈32.902, 97.085 GCJ-02 · Google places · pin approximate

Work up the valley road at walking pace. Partridge and eared pheasant favor the scrubby slopes early and late; laughingthrushes call from the juniper; check the stream for mergansers. When you're done, return to town via the old G214 — about 20 km, birding as you go.

Approximate pin. Lebagou is a valley birded from the road, not a point — the coordinates just mark its mouth near the temple.
