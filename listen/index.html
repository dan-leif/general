    Listen in YouTube Music (or any podcast app)

    Your audio is published as a private podcast. Add this feed once:

    1. Open YouTube Music → Library → Podcasts.
    2. Choose Add podcast / Add RSS feed (the wording varies by app version; on a computer it's at music.youtube.com) and paste the link.
    3. Download episodes for offline listening. Headset buttons, the lock screen and casting to a Google Home / Nest speaker then work like any podcast.

    New episodes can take a while to show up in YouTube Music, because it re-checks feeds on its own schedule. The Ready tab here always has them right away.

    How it works

    1. Every morning a GitHub Action reads your feeds and ranks articles by your interests.
    2. Tick articles and press Make audio. GitHub opens a pre-filled request; press Create.
    3. A second Action reads the articles aloud with a free voice and adds them to your feed. It usually takes a few minutes, longer for very long posts. You'll get a GitHub notification when they're ready.

    Teach it

    Edit your interests (topics to boost, bury or hide) and your feeds (any Substack or site with RSS). Changes apply from the next morning's picks.

    The repo is public, so these pages and files can be seen by anyone who finds them. Don't share the feed link.
