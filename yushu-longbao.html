Longbao — Black-necked Cranes

The S308 runs right across the 4,200 m valley floor — bird from the road, scoping the marsh and pools on both sides. Late August is family season: look for crane pairs trailing one or two rusty-headed chicks. Work the flocks of geese and shelduck for anything hiding among them, and scan the drier margins for a hunting Red Fox.

Approximate pin. Longbao is a valley birded from the road over several kilometres, not a point — the coordinates just put you on the crossing. It's a national reserve: stay on the road corridor and keep distance from the crane families.
