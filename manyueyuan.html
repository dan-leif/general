滿月圓

Mǎnyuèyuán — Full Moon Round

Taxi out at dawn, walk a shaded creek valley through the hottest hours of the day, bus back. About 4 miles on foot, nearly all of it under canopy between 1,000 and 5,600 feet.

Do this first

Photograph the 807 bus board at Parking Lot 2 before you walk in. Six departures a day, and there is no phone signal in the valley to check.

遊客中心 Yóukè Zhōngxīn Visitor center · open today

A mile up the 健行步道 jiànxíng bùdào, flat and shaded. Three halls — butterfly, creek, forest. Free drinking water, restrooms, coffee. Closed Tuesdays, so you've timed it right.

處女瀑布 Chǔnǚ Pùbù Virgin Falls · the reason you came

Eighty feet, a three-story deck, and mist straight in your face. Also called 虹紗瀑布 Hóngshā Pùbù, Rainbow Veil Falls, for what the sun does to the spray. Take your time up here.

One short stretch past 滿足小橋 has storm-fallen trees. Move through briskly, don't stop for photos in it. Open in Maps
10:15
觀瀑亭 Guānpùtíng Manyueyuan Falls viewing pavilion

The trail beyond 滿月小橋 is closed for repairs, but the pavilion is open and sits on your way down. Tall trees have grown across the sightline — expect a partial view through gaps. You'll hear it more than see it.

自導式步道 Zìdǎoshì Bùdào Self-guiding trail · best birding

Three quarters of a mile down through 柳杉 liǔshān cedar plantation. Quieter than the main trail, and the stretch where you're most likely to hear something worth stopping for.

12:15
回程 807 Huíchéng Bus 807 → Sanxia → Dingpu → Taipei

807 to 三峽, 45 minutes. Then the 三鶯線 LRT (free through August 31) or bus 705 to 頂埔, then the Bannan Line to Taipei Main. About 2 hours 15 door to door. Uber becomes reliable once you're in Sanxia town.

The valley

One gate, one creek, two waterfalls. Everything inside the park is on foot — there is no shuttle.

Worth saying out loud
To the taxi driver 我要去三峽滿月圓國家森林遊樂區，第二停車場。 Wǒ yào qù Sānxiá Mǎnyuèyuán guójiā sēnlín yóulèqū, dì-èr tíngchēchǎng. I'm going to Manyueyuan Forest Recreation Area in Sanxia, second parking lot.
At the noodle stall 我不吃麵粉和大蒜，湯裡有嗎？ Wǒ bù chī miànfěn hé dàsuàn, tāng lǐ yǒu ma? I don't eat wheat or garlic — are they in the broth?
At the bus stop 請問回三峽的末班車是幾點？ Qǐngwèn huí Sānxiá de mòbānchē shì jǐ diǎn? What time is the last bus back to Sanxia?
